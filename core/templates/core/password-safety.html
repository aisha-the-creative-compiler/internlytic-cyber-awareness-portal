Password Safety

Learn how to create strong passwords, protect your accounts, and keep your personal information safe online.

1. Why Are Passwords Important?

Passwords help protect your online accounts, personal information, emails, and digital identity. A weak password can make it easier for someone to access an account without permission.

2. How to Create a Strong Password

A strong password should:

  • Be long and difficult to guess.
  • Use a mixture of letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • Avoid your name, birthday, or common words.
  • Be unique for every important account.
Helpful tip: Consider using a long passphrase made from several unrelated words. A password manager can help you create and store unique passwords safely.

3. Avoid Password Reuse

Never use the same password for all your accounts. If one website experiences a data breach, reused passwords could put your other accounts at risk.

Example: Imagine using one password for your email, shopping account, and social media. If that password is exposed, all three accounts could be at risk.

4. What Is Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?

Two-factor authentication adds another security step when you sign in. In addition to your password, you may need a code from an authentication app or another approved method.

  • Enable 2FA on important accounts.
  • Never share verification codes with anyone.
  • Keep your recovery methods secure and updated.

5. Password Safety Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using simple passwords such as 123456.
  • Sharing passwords through messages.
  • Writing passwords where others can see them.
  • Clicking suspicious password-reset links.
  • Sharing one password across multiple websites.

Remember!

Strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication help protect your digital identity.
