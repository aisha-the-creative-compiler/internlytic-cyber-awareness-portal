CYBERSECURITY LEARNING MODULE 01

🎣 Phishing Awareness

Learn how to recognize fraudulent messages, identify warning signs, and protect your personal information.

1. What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cyberattack in which criminals pretend to be trusted people or organizations to trick individuals into revealing sensitive information or clicking harmful links.

These messages may arrive through email, text messages, social media, or other communication platforms.

2. Common Warning Signs

  • Unexpected messages asking for personal information.
  • Urgent threats such as "Your account will be closed!"
  • Spelling mistakes or unusual language.
  • Unknown or suspicious sender addresses.
  • Unexpected attachments or unfamiliar links.
  • Requests for passwords, PINs, or verification codes.

3. Example of a Phishing Message

Subject: URGENT! Account Warning

Hello Customer,

Your fictional account will be suspended today. Send your password and verification code immediately to avoid losing access.

Thank you,

Unknown Support Team

Why is this suspicious?

  • It creates unnecessary urgency.
  • It asks for a password and verification code.
  • The sender is not properly identified.

4. How to Protect Yourself

  • Never share your password or verification codes.
  • Check the sender carefully.
  • Avoid clicking unexpected links.
  • Visit official websites using known addresses.
  • Report suspicious messages to the appropriate organization.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication where available.

5. Remember

Stop, think, and verify before responding to unexpected messages. A few seconds of caution can protect your personal information.
