Suspicious Links Awareness

Learn how to recognize suspicious links, identify fake websites, and protect yourself from online scams.

1. What Is a Suspicious Link?

A suspicious link is a website address that may lead to a fake, harmful, or misleading website. Cybercriminals may use these links to trick people into sharing personal information or downloading harmful files.

2. Warning Signs of Suspicious Links

  • Unexpected messages containing links.
  • Misspelled or unusual website names.
  • Messages creating urgency or fear.
  • Promises of prizes or rewards that seem too good to be true.
  • Requests for passwords or verification codes.
  • Links that do not match the organization they claim to represent.

3. A Fictional Suspicious Message

Example message:

"URGENT! Your account will be closed today. Click the link immediately to verify your password and keep your account active."

This fictional message contains warning signs:

  • It creates unnecessary urgency.
  • It threatens account closure.
  • It asks the recipient to provide a password.

4. How to Check a Link Safely

  • Read the website address carefully before opening it.
  • Check the actual domain name, not just the displayed text.
  • Do not trust a link simply because it uses HTTPS.
  • When in doubt, visit the organization's official website by typing its known address yourself.
  • Contact the organization through an official contact method if a message seems unusual.
Remember: Never enter your password, payment details, or verification code on a website reached through an unexpected message.

5. What Should You Do If You Receive a Suspicious Link?

  • Do not click the link.
  • Do not download unexpected attachments.
  • Do not reply with personal information.
  • Report the message using the platform's reporting feature.
  • Delete or block the sender if appropriate.

Stay Alert. Stay Protected!

Think before you click. A few seconds of checking can help protect your personal information and online accounts.
